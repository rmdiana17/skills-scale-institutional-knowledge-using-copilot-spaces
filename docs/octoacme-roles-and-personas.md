# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Lead

### Role Summary
Delivery Leads coordinate sprint-level execution and keep cross-functional work moving from planning through release. They act as the operational bridge between product priorities, engineering execution, and readiness for delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate sprint planning, dependency tracking, and delivery sequencing
- Identify and remove day-to-day blockers across teams and stakeholders
- Ensure backlog items are ready for development and review
- Own release readiness and milestone confidence for project delivery
- Support escalation paths when work is blocked or at risk

### Goals
- Maintain predictable execution and delivery flow
- Improve handoff quality between planning, build, testing, and release
- Reduce friction caused by unclear ownership or dependencies

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint execution check-ins
- Dependency tracking with PM, development, and QA leads
- Release readiness updates during milestone and weekly planning reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Project Managers on schedules, risks, and stakeholder communication
- Aligns with Product Managers on scope trade-offs, priority changes, and release sequencing
- Coordinates with Developers and QA to keep work flowing and quality gates clear
- Partners with DevOps / Platform Engineering for environment readiness and deployment timing

---

## UX Researcher

### Role Summary
UX Researchers translate customer and user needs into evidence that informs product decisions, feature scope, and usability outcomes. They help the team validate assumptions before and during implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Run lightweight user research and usability testing
- Turn findings into actionable insights and recommendations
- Help shape acceptance criteria around user experience and accessibility
- Identify gaps in understanding before product decisions become expensive
- Partner with design and product stakeholders on experimentation and validation

### Goals
- Improve customer value and usability of the product
- Reduce rework caused by unclear or unvalidated assumptions
- Support decisions with evidence rather than opinion alone

### Typical Communication
- Planning workshops and research synthesis sessions
- User insight reviews with Product Managers and Design partners
- Validation summaries shared in sprint reviews and backlog refinement

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Product Managers on problem framing, success metrics, and user needs
- Provides input to Developers and QA on usability expectations and edge cases
- Supports Project Managers with risk identification when product assumptions are uncertain
- Helps ensure accessibility and user-centered decision-making are represented in work prioritization

---

## Data Analyst

### Role Summary
Data Analysts define how success will be measured and help the team turn product outcomes into actionable signals. They support evidence-based decision-making before launch and after release.

### Responsibilities
- Define measurement plans for product outcomes and feature impact
- Instrument key events, dashboards, and reporting for product performance
- Validate data quality and interpret trends after releases
- Partner with stakeholders to translate metrics into decisions and next steps
- Identify gaps in product data and recommend follow-up analysis

### Goals
- Ensure outcomes are measurable and actionable
- Support product learning and continuous improvement
- Make data quality and reporting part of standard delivery

### Typical Communication
- Metrics reviews with Product Managers and stakeholders
- Data readiness check-ins with Developers and DevOps / Platform Engineering
- Release reporting and post-launch analysis reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to define success metrics and business impact
- Coordinates with Developers and DevOps on instrumentation, event quality, and dashboard readiness
- Helps Project Managers understand whether dependencies or risks affect measurable outcomes
- Provides evidence that supports release validation and backlog prioritization

---

## DevOps / Platform Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Platform Engineers build and maintain the delivery foundations that make software release-safe, repeatable, and observable. They support the team by improving automation, environments, and operational readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Own CI/CD pipelines, deployment automation, and environment setup
- Support runbooks, release procedures, and operational troubleshooting
- Monitor platform health and infrastructure reliability
- Help ensure staging and production parity for testing and release confidence
- Facilitate secure, scalable engineering workflows for the delivery team

### Goals
- Make delivery faster, safer, and more consistent
- Improve release confidence and reduce operational surprises
- Support a stable engineering platform across projects and teams

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment readiness reviews
- Incident and rollback coordination during production issues
- Platform health updates in team syncs and operational review forums

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to improve build, deployment, and environment reliability
- Partners with Delivery Lead to coordinate release timing and rollback preparation
- Supports QA by helping maintain reliable test environments and staging parity
- Collaborates with Security Champion to enforce secure deployment practices and compliance checks

---

## Security Champion

### Role Summary
Security Champions embed security awareness into the delivery process and help the team identify and mitigate risks early. They serve as a bridge between product delivery and secure engineering practices.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security needs during backlog refinement and design reviews
- Support threat modeling, security checklists, and risk triage
- Review security scan findings and drive remediation prioritization
- Coordinate escalation paths for security-sensitive issues and incidents
- Help the team adopt secure patterns without slowing delivery unnecessarily

### Goals
- Reduce security risk without compromising delivery momentum
- Increase security awareness across the team
- Ensure critical risks are surfaced and owned early

### Typical Communication
- Design and planning reviews for sensitive features
- Security triage with Developers, DevOps, and Product stakeholders
- Escalation updates for high-risk issues or incidents

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Developers and DevOps on secure implementation and deployment standards
- Reviews risks with Product Managers before launch decisions or sensitive work starts
- Supports Project Managers with risk prioritization and communication needs
- Elevates issues to broader security or incident response processes when needed
